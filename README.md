One front-page image summing up Wednesday 18 November 2026. The day's
dominant story leads: Ukrainian drones and missiles destroyed the road
arch of the Kerch Bridge — Russia's 19 km link to occupied Crimea, a
flagship project of Vladimir Putin — dropping a 227-metre span into the
Kerch Strait and halting road traffic. Below it, the Gulf escalation:
US jets struck an Iranian Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm
Island, and Iran answered with drones aimed at US sites in Bahrain and
Kuwait. And the diplomacy: Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin
were set to hold the first-ever three-way summit in Shenzhen, on the
sidelines of the APEC meeting. The image is dated 18 November 2026 so
the day it sums up is unmistakable.

`main.py` simply copies the image in this repository to
`/output/entry.png` — deterministic, no network, no randomness.
